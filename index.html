What Hood Fiesta Is All About

A week-long street festival where eight neighbourhoods compete, connect and celebrate, ending with a massive Saturday carnival.

Our Vision

To make Hood Fiesta an annual neighbourhood celebration that promotes unity, healthy competition, youth talent, local business growth and plenty of fun, so every resident looks forward to it each year.

🔥

The Rivalry

Streets go head to head in football, swimming, snooker and table tennis, playing for prizes, trophies and bragging rights.

🤝

The Community

Residents, families, businesses and neighbours meet, cheer together and build stronger bonds across every street.

🎉

The Celebration

The Saturday carnival brings the parade, live DJs, dance, vendors, awards and an after-party that runs till dawn.

🛍️

Business Boost

Local vendors and brands get a big crowd to sell to, showcase their products and meet new customers.

Competition Registration

Four competitions. Eight streets. Pick your game, register your street and play for glory and cash prizes.
